About

From Partnerships To Data, And Back Again

I'm Phuc Nguyen (Phoebe) and my career has been one long argument that partnerships and data should never be handled by separate teams.

I started in tourism and hospitality management at RMIT Vietnam, then moved into B2B sales and account management at TRG International, where I first got hands-on with Salesforce, HubSpot, and InforCRM to make sense of pipeline data. That data instinct carried into enterprise business development at Katalon, where I built pipeline across EMEA and APAC and landed multi-billion-dollar enterprise logos across BFSI, gambling, telecom, energy, and retail.

From September 2024 through August 2026, I was a Global Partner Manager on Amazon Ads' Growth Sales team in Vietnam and the rest of Southeast Asia, where I combined all four of my focus areas at once: I managed strategic partnerships with global and mid-market advertisers, advised them on full-funnel advertising strategy across Amazon DSP, Display, and Video, and on the marketing side used campaign performance data to build expansion strategies. The connection was technology: I spearheaded AudienceIQ, an AI Persona Builder that used Amazon Quick Suite to turn fragmented Seller Central reports into customer personas partners could act on immediately, scaling it from a Vietnam pilot to APAC and EMEA.

I'm now an MS student in Marketing Analytics at California State University, East Bay, going deeper on the statistics and modeling that sat underneath that Amazon Ads work so the next persona tool, pricing model, or attribution study I build is one I can defend with the math, not just the story.
