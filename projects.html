AudienceIQ: AI Persona Builder

An AI tool that turns raw Seller Central data into decision-ready customer personas, piloted across 21 Southeast Asia sellers and scaled to APAC and EMEA.

$8MIncremental revenue
94%Client adoption
45%Less prep time
View live project →
Full breakdown

Leveraged Amazon Quick Suite to transform complex Seller Central data into customer personas with concrete, actionable recommendations: improved CVR, A/B-tested video creative, and ADSP audience targeting.

  • Grounded every persona in real weighted k-means clustering output rather than language-model guesswork.
  • Packaged the workflow as a repeatable three-step process the partner team could run per product.
  • Piloted across 21 Southeast Asia sellers, then scaled to regional rollout across APAC and EMEA.
  • Drove a 34.7% increase in ad sales and a 66.2% reduction in ad spend for one pilot client.
  • Won the AI Champion Award, SEA region, nominated by the SEA Country Manager.

Students Up!: Founding & Partnership-Funded Events

Founded and led a student organization from the ground up, planning skill-building events for high schoolers and securing enterprise sponsorship to fund them.

250+Students engaged
85%Event satisfaction rate
~25M VNDSponsorship secured
12+Enterprise partners
See breakdown below ↓
Full breakdown

Founded Students Up! in 2018 and served as its first President, laying the organization's structure, direction, and membership from scratch, then motivating, training, and mentoring members to run student programs end to end. Mission: motivate high school students to strive for excellence, believe in themselves, and think outside the box so they contribute more meaningful action to their community.

  • Planned and ran a 3-part workshop and talk-show series in 2 months, built to sharpen students' public speaking, teamwork, and time management.
  • Engaged 250+ students across the series, with an 85% satisfaction rate in post-event survey forms.
  • Secured sponsorship and media support from 12+ enterprise partners — including VMG Centre, Lion Coffee, and Ybox — totaling nearly 25 million VND, to fund event execution.
  • Recruited and hosted 3 CEOs, 2 professors, and 3 social influencers as guest speakers and mentors, including moderating a guest panel with Duy Khiêm, CEO of 5S Investment JSC, at a VMG Centre & IDP-partnered study-abroad info session.
